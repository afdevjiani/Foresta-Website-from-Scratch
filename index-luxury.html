Our Story

45 Years of Excellence

Since our inception, Foresta Wood Industries has embodied the perfect synthesis of time-honored craftsmanship and contemporary innovation.

Positioned strategically at the world's busiest port, we've grown into one of the fastest-expanding manufacturers in the MENA region. Our journey is defined not by scale alone, but by an unwavering commitment to quality that transforms spaces into experiences.

Every panel we craft carries the weight of 45 years of expertise, refined through countless projects that have shaped the architectural landscape of the region.
